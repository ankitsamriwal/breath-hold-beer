Privacy

Hold My Beer - last updated September 2026

What we hold

Free players: nothing. The free game runs entirely on your device. Your best score lives in your browser's local storage and never leaves your phone.

Signed-in players: your email address (used only to send your sign-in link), the display name you pick, and your leaderboard scores (hold duration, chosen glass and beer, timestamps).

Premium players: payment is handled entirely by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. We never see or store your card number. We receive only a confirmation that you paid, your email, and a payment reference.

What we never do

No ads, no trackers, no analytics pixels, no sale or sharing of your data with anyone. Emails are used for sign-in links only - never marketing.

Where it lives

Account and leaderboard data is stored with Supabase (Mumbai region). Authentication and database are protected by row-level security; scores are written only by our server after timing your hold itself.

Delete everything

Want out? One email deletes your account, your scores, and your sign-in record. Contact the maker through the site you came from and it's done within 72 hours.

Safety

This is a breath-hold game with a virtual pint. Never hyperventilate before a breath hold, never practise breath-holding in or near water, and stop the second you feel dizzy. It measures CO2 tolerance - it is not a medical or fitness test. If you pour a real one, legal drinking age applies.
